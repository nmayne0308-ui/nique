Hurricane Ready

Go-Kit: 3+ days, portable. Stay-at-Home Kit: 2+ weeks. Quantities scale with household size (set in Plan).

Alerts come from the US National Weather Service. They need internet; the last result is kept for offline viewing. Always confirm with local officials.

Location (for alerts)

Disaster Distress Helpline: call or text 1-800-985-5990. Emergencies: 911.

Based on the American Red Cross Hurricane Preparedness Checklist. Wording here is paraphrased; see the original and the Red Cross Emergency App at redcross.org/prepare. This app is a memory aid, not a substitute for instructions from local officials.
